**Checked 11 November 2026.** The demo covers banking AML, CFT and Cybersecurity in both markets from **4 November 2025 through 4 November 2026 inclusive**. This plan implements the scope in the [PRD](../mira_PRD_v3_draft.md#74-demo-market-scope-and-readiness). Costs below are in USD. Discovery and read-only database inspection were used to size the work.

## What counts as covered

Include substantive official rules, guidance, consultations and responses, enforcement, material sanctions and counter-terrorist financing updates, and cybersecurity advisories and official incident disclosures applicable to banks. Include an in-window commencement even if the instrument was published earlier. Keep earlier rules that applied as baselines and analyse the sections relevant to the in-window developments or report. Mark consultation, final publication and commencement as separate events. Keep bank applicability explicit for rules addressed only to a subset of banks or designated infrastructure operators.

Use separate AML, CFT and Cybersecurity report labels. Analyse genuinely shared AML/CFT passages once and use only the findings relevant to each report. Treat proliferation financing, sanctions and AML as distinct from CFT unless the text establishes a CFT connection. Routine individual designation updates, generic technical bulletins, material solely for non-bank audiences and commercial news sit outside the required corpus. Secondary news is optional and permission-dependent.

For each source category and month, reconcile an inventory against the official publication and version lists. Each row should record canonical URL, publisher ID, audience, topic tags, publication and effective dates, parent and annex links, eligibility and reason, permission, exact version and hash, extraction and analysis status, and any unresolved gap. Record zero relevant changes only after checking the category. Retain one usable official language version per publication and flag substantive differences that warrant a separate version. Keep source archives and substantive publications distinguishable so that an index or press release cannot stand in for the underlying instrument.

## Lower-cost plan for broad coverage

1. **Index the full corpus.** Build the official inventory for both markets and all three topics. Preserve permitted originals, extract the full text and make it searchable with PostgreSQL keyword indexing at **$0 in model charges**. Track access and extraction gaps. [Current search index](../../supabase/migrations/20261012220000_mira_initial_schema.sql)
2. **Find candidate facts across the full text.** Pass every eligible substantive passage through a low-cost extractor. Store each distinct candidate with its source span, dates, applicability, topic and whether it is a requirement, proposal, enforcement outcome or reported allegation. A single publication can produce several unrelated candidates. Record passages where extraction found none and retry truncated or failed work. Use deterministic version matching to reuse unchanged results and skip duplicate content, with enough neighbouring context to interpret changes and cross-references. Keep publisher identity so duplicate coverage does not count as independent corroboration.
3. **Verify candidates before they enter a report.** Check spans, dates and duplication locally where possible, then use the semantic verifier for meaning and support. Verify material developments for each market/topic first. Keep other candidates marked awaiting verification and visible as coverage gaps. Retain exact revised citations, check inputs and inference premises. If a report needs more checks, show the known gap and expected wait. Ready-evidence report assembly stays model-free.
