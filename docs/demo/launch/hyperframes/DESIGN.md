# Mira motion identity

Approved Follow the evidence direction. Source: the existing Mira PowerPoint and the approved storytelling design.

## Style prompt

Evidence fills the screen. The camera follows a real citation into an official publication and returns to the report. White page surfaces, large charcoal type and one red focus per shot connect the deck and film. Motion follows the source, passage or reviewer action. Product screens and publications remain authentic, with explicit synthetic chapter labels where required.

## Colors

- White #FFFFFF: page/stage
- Charcoal #222222: titles and primary copy
- Grey #60646B: secondary copy
- Red #E60028: citation, selected evidence and transitions

## Typography

Arial or bundled compatible sans; display 72–104px, supporting copy 32–40px, disclosure minimum28px. 96px action margin, critical text within 80% title safe.

## Motion

One hero move per shot; 12–18frame camera moves, 12–21frame entrances, 3–6frame stagger. Ease power2.inOut for camera, power3.out/expo.out for entry. Type stays still while read. Crop and match positions make transitions follow evidence.

## What not to do

- No invented UI, source document or performance claims.
- No dark floating UI, glow, particles, decorative cards or persistent corner logo.
- No text drifting while read, repeated slide-up entrances or unreadable screenshots.
- Do not erase disclosures while cropping or imply that authentic HK findings passed through the synthetic fixture.
