## Agentic AI for Audit: External Driver Researcher

Sponsor: DBS Bank | Business Domain: Internal Audit Coordinators: Wei Wu, Ella Ngiam | Mentors: Huayu Wu, Wei Wang

### Overview & Problem Statement

The internal audit function provides independent assurance on governance, risk management, and controls, requiring auditors to review significant volumes of structured and unstructured data. Rapidly evolving regulatory and industry changes make manual monitoring inefficient and difficult to scale.  
This project seeks to build an AI-powered agentic solution to streamline intelligence gathering, improve awareness of emerging developments, and support proactive decision-making.

### Scope of Research & Synthesis

The solution must research and synthesize information across locations for a given topic (e.g., AML, Cybersecurity, Data Privacy) to produce a comprehensive report covering:

#### A. Regulatory Developments

* **Monitor:** Track announcements, consultation papers, guidelines, enforcement actions, and policy updates from regulators across selected markets.  
* **Identify & Summarize:** Flag new, amended, or upcoming regulations and summarize key obligations in business-friendly language.  
* **Track Timelines:** Highlight effective dates, implementation timelines, and compliance deadlines.  
* **Assess Impact:** Evaluate potential risks, opportunities, and required actions for the organization.  
* **Compare Jurisdictions:** Identify common themes and regional differences.  
* **Analyze Trends:** Track regulatory trends and emerging supervisory focus areas.  
* **Verify:** Provide source references and direct links to original regulatory publications.

#### B. Industry Developments

* **Monitor:** Track industry news, publications, major market events, incidents, and case studies.

**Identify:** Spot emerging trends, best practices, and industry innovations.

### Inputs & Deliverables

#### Project Inputs

* List of sample topics to focus on.  
* Based on internet data.

Project Deliverables  
Develop an AI-powered agentic solution with an intuitive user interface that enables users to:

* Select a topic (e.g., AML, Cybersecurity, Data Privacy).  
* Define jurisdictions/regions of interest.  
* Generate a structured report summarizing relevant regulatory and industry developments.  
* Implement a multi-agent workflow to autonomously research, analyze, synthesize, and consolidate information from authoritative sources.  
* Incorporate robust guardrails to ensure:  
  * Factual accuracy and source-grounded responses with traceability back to source.  
  * Completeness and comprehensive coverage of selected jurisdictions and topics.

Detection and mitigation of hallucinations or unsupported claims.
