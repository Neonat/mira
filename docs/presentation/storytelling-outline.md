**Purpose:** Explain Mira to a DBS audit audience through one evidence-led story, then give technical and evaluation detail in six appendices.

**Evidence cut:** 7 November 2026. The deck separates current prototype behavior, approved future work and unproven targets. It does not claim independent corroboration, comprehensive coverage, auditor-labelled precision or measured time savings.
