# Hong Kong AML/CFT research sample

This portfolio export presents one paraphrased finding for each of the 18 benchmark publications cited by Mira’s final Hong Kong demo draft. The research period was **4 November 2025 through 4 November 2026**. Dates below are the publishers’ issue dates. For the FATF updates, the dates refer to the SFC circulars that communicated them in Hong Kong.

The underlying report was a proof-of-concept draft with human evidence review pending. The 18 publications are the scope of the project benchmark. [Demo validation and measurement scope](../../eval/evidence/2026-11-12-hk-poc-inference-download.md)

| Published | Paraphrased finding | Publisher source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-11-07 | HKMA set out when authorized institutions may share information to help detect or prevent crime, including the conditions for disclosure without the affected party’s consent. | [HKMA · Interbank information-sharing guideline](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251106-2-EN/20251106-2-EN.pdf#page=24) |
| 2025-11-17 | The SFC described signs of possible layering involving licensed firms and virtual-asset platforms and reiterated its expectations for controls that detect and prevent it. | [SFC · Layering circular, 25EC62](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=25EC62) |
| 2025-11-19 | HKMA reported that 48 authorized institutions had assessed AI for transaction monitoring and that more than 30% had incorporated it into their monitoring systems. | [HKMA · AI adoption for AML/CFT](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251118-3-EN/20251118-3-EN.pdf#page=1) |
| 2025-11-21 | HKMA found examples of controls for politically exposed persons that were heavier than the risk warranted. It published guidance to help authorized institutions and stored value facility licensees calibrate those controls. | [HKMA · Politically exposed persons guidance](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251118-4-EN/20251118-4-EN.pdf#page=1) |
| 2025-12-12 | HKMA highlighted checking where higher-risk customers’ wealth and funds came from as an important preventive control. It expected institutions to calibrate those checks to the customer’s risk. | [HKMA · High-end money laundering guidance](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20251210-1-EN/20251210-1-EN.pdf#page=2) |
| 2025-12-24 | The agencies said they would take forward legislation to license virtual-asset dealing and custody providers. They opened a further consultation on advisory and management services. | [FSTB and SFC · Virtual-asset licensing update, 25PR210](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=25PR210) |
| 2026-02-09 | The SFC reprimanded and fined Kylin International (HK) HK$9 million over private fund management failures. The findings included gaps in records that would show compliance with AML/CFT requirements. | [SFC · Kylin enforcement release, 26PR19](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR19) |
| 2026-03-13 | The SFC relayed FATF’s continued call for enhanced due diligence proportionate to the risk arising from Myanmar. FATF said it would consider countermeasures in June if further progress fell short. | [SFC · February FATF update, 26EC13](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=26EC13) |
| 2026-03-16 | HKMA found that most authorized institutions in its review regularly reported sanctions-screening performance to senior management. It advised setting the frequency of system testing according to risk and past test results. | [HKMA · Sanctions-screening thematic review](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20260316-1-EN/20260316-1-EN.pdf#page=2) |
| 2026-06-22 | HKMA asked authorized institutions with significant Hong Kong operations that had submitted AI implementation plans to keep those plans under review and report progress and potential new uses when asked. | [HKMA · AI adoption to fight financial crime](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20260622-1-EN/20260622-1-EN.pdf#page=1) |
| 2026-06-23 | Wolfsberg recommends directing resources toward the financial-crime risks that matter most and scaling back controls that do little to reduce them. | [Wolfsberg Group · Risk-based approach](https://wolfsberg-group.org/news/guidance-on-the-risk-based-approach) |
| 2026-07-14 | When circulating FATF’s June update, the SFC reminded the firms it regulates to give extra scrutiny to relationships and transactions linked to higher-risk jurisdictions. | [SFC · June FATF update, 26EC39](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=26EC39) |
| 2026-07-15 | Wolfsberg recommends adapting due diligence and monitoring to the risks of the services each non-bank payment provider actually offers. | [Wolfsberg Group · Banking services for payment providers](https://wolfsberg-group.org/news/guidance-on-the-provision-of-banking-services) |
| 2026-07-24 | The SFC said Victory Securities inadequately investigated warning signs in a client account before executing orders and later failed to notify the regulator about suspected false documents. | [SFC · Victory Securities enforcement release, 26PR113](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR113) |
| 2026-08-28 | HKMA released revised examples for assessing politically exposed persons and asked authorized institutions and stored value facility licensees to consider them when improving risk-based controls. | [HKMA · Updated politically exposed persons guidance](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20260827-4-EN/20260827-4-EN.pdf#page=1) |
| 2026-09-03 | HKMA updated its expectations for opening individual accounts remotely, citing impersonation and mule-account risks linked to deepfakes. The circular replaced two earlier individual remote onboarding circulars from 2019 and 2021. | [HKMA · Remote onboarding of individual customers](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20260902-1-EN/20260902-1-EN.pdf#page=1) |
| 2026-09-29 | The SFC fined Zheng Da HK$7 million after finding that it had failed to assess customer-supplied trading systems and had ineffective controls for monitoring suspicious trading. | [SFC · Zheng Da enforcement release, 26PR158](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR158) |
| 2026-10-22 | HKMA called for risk-based reviews of accounts opened with help from third-party agents to identify possible bribery or corruption in the opening process. | [HKMA · Anti-bribery controls for account opening](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20261022-1-EN/20261022-1-EN.pdf#page=3) |
